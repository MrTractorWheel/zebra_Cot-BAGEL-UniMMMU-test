We also know that AB is the diameter and D is the midpoint of arc AB, so ∠ACD=90° (angle in a semicircle). Since OC=OD (radii of the same circle), ∠OCD=∠ODC. Since ∠ACD=90°, ∠ACE=90°-∠OCD. Since ∠OCF=90°, ∠ECF=90°-∠OCD. So ∠ACE=∠ECF. Since ∠ECF=∠EFC (alternate angles as CF is tangent and CD is a chord), ∠ACE=∠EFC. Therefore, ∠ACE=∠EFC, which means triangle CEF is isosceles with CF=EF.</think>
