<think>The problem asks for the measure of ∠BPC. Since ABCD is an inscribed square, its vertices lie on the circle. The square implies that ∠BCD is 90°. Point P is on the minor arc CD. By the Inscribed Angle Theorem, the measure of an inscribed angle is half the measure of its intercepted arc. ∠BPC intercepts arc BC. Since ABCD is a square, arc BC is 90°. Therefore, ∠BPC = 1/2 * arc BC = 1/2 * 90° = 45°.</think>
